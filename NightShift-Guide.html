NightShift

Keep your laptop awake with the lid closed - for timed agent sessions.
The owl turns amber while your session runs  ·  no admin rights needed

What is this?

Your laptop is trained to sleep the moment you close the lid. Great for battery - terrible when something is working for you in the background.

The 2-hour problem. You tell your AI coding agent (Claude Code, Codex): "Build this feature, write the tests, fix anything broken." That's a 2-hour job. You close the lid and walk away - Windows puts the laptop to sleep within seconds, the agent freezes mid-task, and you come back to half-done work.

With NightShift: click the owl, set 0d 2h 0m, press Enter, close the lid. The laptop stays awake with the lid closed, the agent works the full two hours, and when the timer ends the laptop goes back to sleep on its own. You come back to finished work.

It's for any "close the lid and walk away" job: long agent runs, big downloads, video renders, training jobs. When no session is running, NightShift does nothing at all - Windows behaves exactly as it always did.

1How to use it - three gestures

You want to…Do this
Start a sessionClick the owl → type days / hours / minutes → Enter
Add more timeClick the owl again → type more time, or tap +15m +30m +1h +6h
Stop earlyClick the owl → press and hold the red button for 1.5 s
Hold to end
Deliberately slow - a quick click can't end your session by accident.

Right-click the owl for the menu: status & countdown, add time, end early, Relaunch browser unthrottled, Settings, logs, Quit. Quitting mid-session asks first and undoes everything.

2What happens inside

1
Start borrows Windows' power settings - and leaves a note. It sets never sleep when idle, never hibernate when idle, and lid close → do nothing. Before changing anything, it writes every change and its undo command to a journal file. Like borrowing a bike and leaving a note: "I took it, here's how to return it."
2
Every 15 seconds it taps the note: "still alive." This heartbeat is how the app proves it's running.
3
Lid closed = quiet, not asleep. Screen off (brightness 0), audio muted, and optionally Slack / WhatsApp / Discord get truly frozen - a pause button - so nothing wakes the laptop. Your agent keeps running at full speed; the timer keeps counting.
4
Timer ends = everything goes back. Brightness, sound, frozen apps, power settings - restored exactly. And since the lid is still closed with no external display, the laptop puts itself to sleep.

3The safety net

  • Crash-proof. A helper task checks every minute: "journal says a session is live, but the app is dead?" Then it reads the note and undoes every change by itself - your laptop can never get stuck awake.
  • Battery guard. Low-power mode below 40%, session ends below 10% (both configurable, 0 disables).
  • Heat guard. Gets hot → low power. Critically hot → session ends.
  • Protected quit & shutdown. Logoff, shutdown, or Quit all restore your settings first.

4Good to know

Zero admin rightsWindows lets a normal user change lid & sleep settings. NightShift needs no elevation, no SDK, no runtime install.
App freezing is opt-inOff by default because it pauses real apps. Turn it on in Settings → lid-close actions.
Browser unthrottledRestarts a running Chromium browser (Chrome/Edge/Brave/Arc…) with background throttling off, tabs restored - for web-based agents.
Modern Standby readyWorks on S0 and classic S3 laptops - it even reads Windows' hidden lid settings, which some laptops require.

5Install & uninstall

powershell -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File .\install.ps1

Builds the app, installs it, registers the recovery task, adds the startup shortcut, and starts the owl in your tray.

powershell -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File .\uninstall.ps1           # keeps settings
powershell -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File .\uninstall.ps1 -Purge   # wipes everything

Requires Windows 10 (1709+) or Windows 11. Data & logs live in %LOCALAPPDATA%\NightShift.

6FAQ

  • Does it work when I'm at the desk, lid open? You don't need it then - it only changes Windows' behavior while a session is running, and reverts everything after.
  • What if the power dies mid-session? On next boot, the recovery task reads the journal and restores your settings within a minute of login.
  • Is it safe to run all night? The guards end the session on low battery or critical heat. Still: keep the laptop on a hard, ventilated surface - not a closed bag.
  • Desktop PC? Works as an idle-sleep blocker; lid features simply don't apply.
